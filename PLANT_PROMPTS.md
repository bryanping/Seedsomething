# Plant Image Generation Prompts

This document contains prompts for generating 50 plant illustrations in 5 growth stages each.

**Style:** Soft pastel watercolor, minimal, no outline, centered, transparent background.

## Prompt Template

```
Create a soft watercolor-style illustration inspired by the real plant "{{PLANT_NAME}}".

Visual style:
- pastel watercolor
- minimal and abstract
- low contrast
- rounded and friendly shapes
- calm and gentle feeling
- no outline

Design intent:
- symbolic, not realistic
- suitable for a companion-style mobile app
- icon-friendly silhouette
- visually simple and recognizable

Composition:
- single plant only
- centered
- balanced proportions
- non background 

Do not include:
- pot
- people
- animals
- text
- strong shadows
- realistic textures

Stage specific details: {{STAGE_DESCRIPTION}}
```
